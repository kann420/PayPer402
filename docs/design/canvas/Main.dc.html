OPEN STANDARD

402 finally means something.

HTTP reserved 402 Payment Required in 1997. The open x402 standard lets a server name a price and a client pay and retry, all inside HTTP. PayPer402 is the first PayPal rail for it.

Read the binding spec View on GitHub
server.js
import { paymentMiddleware } from "@x402/express";

// $0.02 per call, settled with PayPal

  "GET /v1/quote": { accepts: { scheme: "batch-settlement", network: "paypal:sandbox",

}, resourceServer));

Questions

Do I need crypto?+

No. No wallets, tokens or seed phrases. Payments settle in US dollars through PayPal.

What does the agent need?+

An agent key from your console and the standard x402 fetch client with our PayPal plug-in. It handles the 402, signs and retries.

How are fees kept low?+

Paid calls are batched into one PayPal charge at your auto-charge amount, or once a day.

Can I stop it?+

Yes. Unlink here, or stop from your PayPal account. Agents are refused within a minute.

Who receives the money?+

Each PayPal charge is made by PayPer402. Sellers see their share of every charge, after PayPal's fee.

Who holds my PayPal details?+

PayPal does. We only keep a token for the agreement you approved.
